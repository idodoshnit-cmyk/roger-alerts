Privacy notice

This notice explains how Painters and Decorators Leicester JK (“we”) uses personal information under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

We are the data controller. Contact us at Unit 1L04, 90 Sanvey Gate, Leicester LE1 4BQ, or call 0116 478 5346.

What we collect

When you send a quote request: your name, phone number, postcode and details of the job. When you become a customer we also keep your address, quotes and invoices.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote: taking steps at your request before entering a contract.
  • To carry out the work and invoice you: performance of a contract.
  • To keep financial records: legal obligation.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only with providers that help us run the business, such as our form, email and accounting services, and only as far as needed. Our quote form is handled by Formspree, which may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted after 12 months. Customer and invoice records are kept for 6 years to meet HMRC requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
