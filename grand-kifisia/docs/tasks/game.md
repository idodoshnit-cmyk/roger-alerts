### What to build
1. Movement, camera and controls that feel excellent on a phone (this is the owner's most repeated complaint):
   - Analogue movement with natural acceleration/deceleration, turning that follows the stick without snapping, sliding along walls instead of sticking, smooth stepping on and off 16 cm curbs and platform edges, slopes handled without jitter, no vibration when idle against geometry, frame-rate independent (test with dt of 1/30 and 1/120 by driving update() yourself in a scripted test).
   - A camera that never jerks: critically damped follow, look-ahead in the direction of travel, smooth obstacle avoidance (pull in and raise instead of clipping through walls, ease back out), sensible framing indoors (player.interior set) and in cars (the cars agent owns car physics; you own the camera — make the driving camera cinematic: speed-based distance and field of view, slight lag on turns, shake on impacts via the existing camShake).
   - Touch: forgiving joystick dead zone and response curve, auto-run at full deflection with a sprint toggle, drag-look with inertia and adjustable sensitivity, buttons large enough for thumbs, safe-area aware, multi-touch correct (move + look + button at once), no accidental page scroll/zoom/selection, works in portrait and landscape. Keyboard + mouse on desktop (WASD, Shift, Space, F, E, J, M, Esc, mouse-drag look, wheel zoom). Options: sensitivity, invert Y, left-handed layout.
2. HUD in the visual language of a AAA open-world crime game without copying any existing game: radar-style minimap that rotates with the camera, with the street network, blips, and a GPS route line drawn along the real road graph (ROADS / ROADNODES) to the current objective or to a waypoint the player sets on the map; street/district name; cash with animated changes; health; clock; objective text; subtitles for speech with the speaker's name; contextual prompts. Everything readable on a small phone in sunlight, nothing overlapping the controls.
3. A real menu system: title/main menu on top of the existing photo slideshow (Continue / New game / Settings / Credits), and an in-game pause menu with tabs — MAP (full map that can be panned and pinch/wheel zoomed, legend, every special place and mission labelled, tap to set a waypoint → GPS route), BRIEF (missions: available / active / done, with rewards), STATS (money earned, distance walked/driven, missions passed, people talked to…), SETTINGS (graphics preset, controls, audio volume, show FPS), CREDITS (from docs/CREDITS.md content you embed). The map must look good: drawn from MAP data with clear streets, building footprints, parks, the railway, labels for main streets in Latin letters, icons for shops/stations.
4. A mission framework and polished missions that "really complete and pay out":
   - data-driven steps (go to, interact, wait for, timed, stealth meter, mini-game), checkpoints, fail and retry without reloading, an intro card and a short establishing camera move, objective updates, a "MISSION PASSED" moment with the reward counted into the wallet and saved, mission replay from the BRIEF tab. No dead ends: every mission can always be finished, failed or quit.
   - Missions (keep the three, make them great, and add the owner's others): (a) Gyro Run at Mythos Plus — if the interiors module is present (typeof enterInterior === 'function' / INTERIORS.mythos) the player walks INTO Mythos and orders at the counter; otherwise at the door as now; (b) After Hours at ISA — climb the fence, avoid the guard's torch, three baskets with a timing mini-game that feels good, escape; (c) Line 1 to the Mall — ticket, validate, board when the train arrives; (d) Walk to School — a daytime mission that walks the real route from the centre to the International School of Athens (MAP.route) before the bell, with GPS guidance and commentary on real streets passed; (e) A Gyro for a Stranger — the homeless man near the cosmetics store (a CAST character from the chars agent; feature-detect and fall back to a simple seated NPC you spawn at MAP.key.sephora or, if absent, near the "Hondos center" store at about x=268, z=-1): talk to him, buy food at Mythos, bring it back; warm, respectful writing; (f) one driving mission that will showcase the exotic cars when they exist (feature-detect CARTYPES for a supercar; otherwise any car): e.g. deliver a customer's car from Kassaveti to a hotel near Kefalari without scratching it, with a damage meter and a timer.
   - Mission givers/markers at real places; rewards in euro; a believable economy with the shop prices.
5.
