## Your workstream: vehicles that look and drive right, and traffic that behaves

You own: gk3/js/70_vehicles.js, gk3/js/72_driving.js, gk3/js/76_traffic.js, gk3/js/83_audio.js, docs/VEHICLES.md. You may edit the few lines in boot() (js/90_main.js) that create the car material, parked cars and traffic.

### What exists now (read it first)
- 70_vehicles.js: boxy procedural cars (hatch, sedan, suv, taxi, van) drawn as instanced meshes with one MeshStandardMaterial (vertex colours, small generated env map); CARS records {type,x,z,yaw,vf,vr,steer,mode:'parked'|'traffic'|'free'|'player',color,…}; carPush() collision; a simple box train on the Line 1 track (TRAIN, placeTrain, updateTrain).
- 72_driving.js: arcade bicycle-model physics for the player's car, enterCar/exitCar/nearestCar. 76_traffic.js: 16 cars follow road centre lines (lane offset), stop at red lights and behind obstacles, respawn out of range. 83_audio.js: one sawtooth oscillator engine and beeps.
- Other modules use: CARS, CARTYPES[c.type].L/.W, addCar, syncCar, flushCars, carPush, nearestCar, enterCar, exitCar, driveCar, TRAIN, placeTrain, updateTrain, engine(), sfx(), audio(). Keep these working (or patch call sites minimally and list them).
- The owner's verdict: not realistic, not smooth, "should feel like a AAA open-world crime game".

### What to build
1. Car models with real proportions and curved bodywork, generated procedurally (lofted cross-sections along the car, proper wheel arches, bonnet/roof/boot curvature, A/B/C pillars, inset glass, bumpers, mirrors, door and panel seams, grille, light clusters) — no third-party car meshes are available, so the modelling craft is yours. The Athens street mix: superminis and small hatchbacks (the majority), a compact saloon as the yellow Athens taxi with roof sign, compact and large SUVs and a couple of premium cars (Kifisia is wealthy), a delivery van, a blue-and-white city bus, scooters and motorbikes (parked everywhere in rows, and ridden, including delivery riders with a top box). Greek number plates (white with the blue EU strip, three letters and four digits, as text drawn on a canvas tile).
   - The owner explicitly asked: "put in a Ferrari and crazy cars that you can drive". Deliver a set of exotic, drivable cars parked at believable spots (outside the hotels and villas toward Kefalari, on Kassaveti, near the spawn junction), each with its own handling, top speed and engine note: (a) the Ferrari 458 model that ships with the three.js examples: https://threejs.org/examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb (Draco-compressed: decode it offline — e.g. gltf-transform with the draco3d npm package — simplify it to the car budget, bake/keep its paint/glass/wheel parts so the wheels can spin and the paint colour can vary, and ship it as .json/.jpg; read the model's licence/credit from the three.js example "webgl_materials_car" and the glb metadata and record it in docs/CREDITS.md); (b) at least three more supercars/luxury cars modelled by you with the same lofting craft (a low wedge-shaped supercar, a rear-engined German-style sports coupé, a boxy luxury off-roader, a convertible). Also check Poly Haven models (https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?t=models) for any usable CC0 vehicles or scooters. Give the cars neutral in-game names plus the real model name only where the asset itself is that model (the Ferrari).
   - Wheels with tyres and alloy rims that rotate with speed and steer at the front; glass that is dark, slightly transparent and reflective, with a simple interior (seats, wheel, dashboard) visible behind it; head, tail, brake, reverse and indicator lights that work and glow at night, with soft headlight pools on the road.
   - Budget: near LOD ≤ ~2,500 triangles per car, far LOD ≤ ~300; everything instanced (bodies, wheels, glass) with per-instance colour; about 480 parked cars exist in the map plus ~40 moving vehicles; parked cars far away must cost almost nothing. Do not exceed the frame budgets in ARCHITECTURE.md.
   - The Line 1 train: model it as the real Athens ISAP stock as far as public descriptions allow (white/cream bodies with coloured stripe, sliding double doors, cab ends, headlights, bogies), with doors that open at the platform.
2. Driving that feels good on a phone and a keyboard: responsive but weighty arcade physics (traction and slip, weight transfer shown as pitch and roll on a sprung body, handbrake turns, kerb bumps, terrain following on Kifisia's hills, reversing), automatic gearbox with audible shifts, speed-sensitive steering, collisions with walls, props, other cars (they get shoved and can be dented in a simple way, e.g. bumper/bonnet deformation or at least sparks, glass and a damage state with smoke) and with people. No snagging on walls, no tunnelling at speed, no jitter when parked on a slope.
   - Entering and leaving: the door opens and closes (make the door a separate articulated part for the car the player is using), the car settles on its springs. Provide a hook (documented) for the characters agent to seat a driver/passengers: attachDriver(car, person) / seat positions per car type. Until then show a simple seated driver silhouette inside moving traffic cars.
3. Sound in 83_audio.js, synthesised with WebAudio (no audio files): a layered engine (several oscillators + filtered noise, rpm from speed and gear, load from throttle), tyre squeal when slipping, wind at speed, door, horn (distinct for car/scooter/bus), crash and scrape, indicator tick, scooter buzz, the train (rumble, door chime, the Line 1 departure tone). Positional attenuation by distance for traffic. All sound starts only after the first user gesture (audio() is called from input handlers).
4. Traffic that respects the real street network in 76_traffic.js: lane discipline on the proper side (right-hand traffic), oneway streets, smooth curved paths through junctions (no snapping), traffic lights and stop signs (see SIGNALS and MAP.props.stop in 50_props.js), giving way to pedestrians on crossings (NPCS that are crossing) and to the player, sensible following distance, indicators before turns, taxis that pull in at the Platanos rank, a bus that runs along Kifisias Avenue and stops at the real bus stops (MAP.props.bus), scooters filtering past queues, the odd double-parked car with hazard lights. Vehicles must never appear or disappear in view: spawn and despawn beyond the fog distance or behind the camera. Density varies with the time of day (tod in 82_sky.js).
5. docs/VEHICLES.md: the public API, CARS record fields, how to add a vehicle type, the seat/door hooks, audio functions, tuning constants.

### How to verify (do all of it)
- Beauty shots of every vehicle type at 3-5 m (front three-quarter, side, rear, from above), by day and at night with lights on; a row of parked scooters; the taxi rank; the train at the platform with doors open.
- A scripted drive: enter the nearest car (enterCar()), set input.jy=-1 to accelerate, steer with input.jx, capture frames every 300 ms for several seconds on a straight, through a junction turn, a handbrake turn (input.brake), a wall hit and a hill, and inspect the body attitude, wheel rotation/steer, lights, camera framing and the car's path (log speed/yaw/position to check for jitter).
- A traffic watch: top-down free camera 60 m above two junctions for 20+ seconds of game time sampled every second: no car leaves its lane, drives through another, runs a red light or teleports.
- Stats: triangles and draw calls with many cars in view, by quality preset.
