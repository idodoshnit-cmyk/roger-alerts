## Your role: IMPLEMENT BOT — streets and public realm, built exactly as the researchers report

You turn data and research into the world the player walks through: ground, roads, sidewalks, walls, vegetation, street furniture, the railway and station area.
You own: gk3/js/20_ground.js, gk3/js/40_walls.js, gk3/js/45_trees.js, gk3/js/50_props.js, gk3/js/55_rail.js, docs/STREETS_IMPL.md. (INTER, ROADS, SPECIAL and other shared state are declared in 20_ground.js: keep those declarations.)

Inputs you must keep re-reading at every milestone (they are being written while you work): gk3/data/streets.json and places.json (research specs — build what they say, street by street), docs/research/STREETS.md, PLACES.md and GAPS_STREETS.md, docs/MAP_DATA.md (the data agent is adding every real bench, bin, bus stop, post box, bollard, gate, steps, crossing type, tree and calibrated street widths under new map keys — feature-detect them and use real data instead of synthetic placement wherever it exists), docs/MATERIALS.md (the materials agent is replacing the tile atlas with real photo materials; T.<name>/RECT[...] keep working and new names appear — use them), docs/ORDERS.md (commander).

What to build
1. Roads that look real: correct width per street (from data/research), lane markings as in Greece (white centre/edge lines, stop lines, zebra crossings at the mapped crossings, yellow no-parking curb lines, arrows on one-way streets, bus lanes/taxi bays where reported), asphalt variety (patches, manholes, drains at the curb, worn zones at junctions), speed humps, parking bays, correct junction geometry with real corner radii and dropped curbs at crossings, tactile paving where mapped.
2. Sidewalks and plazas per street spec (red brick, grey pavers, marble, slabs), curbs with real profile, tree pits (with grates on shopping streets), driveway crossovers, steps (highway=steps) built as real stairs, ramps, retaining walls on the slopes, pedestrian streets.
3. Property edges: stone and plaster garden walls with iron railings, gates and gate pillars at real entrances, hedges, bougainvillea on walls — matching the street spec; never in front of a shopfront.
4. Vegetation with species per street from the research (plane trees on the avenues and squares, bitter-orange trees on sidewalks, pines/cypress/palms/olives in gardens and the Alsos): convincing trunks and crowns (branching structure + alpha-cut leaf cards from the photo foliage textures), size variety, gentle wind sway in the vertex shader, shadows, cheap far LOD. The big plane tree of Platanos square as a landmark.
5. Street furniture, each modelled as the real local design from the research (dimensions, colours): lamp post models per street, bollards, benches, litter bins, big wheeled municipal bins, kiosks (periptero) with fridges/stands/awning, bus shelters and stop poles with the stop name, the taxi rank at Platanos with its sign, post boxes, phone booths, ATMs on walls, bike/scooter parking, street-name plates (blue, Greek + Latin), traffic lights and road signs (stop, give way, one-way, no entry, parking), planters, café terrace sets (tables, chairs, umbrellas, planters, menu stands) on the forecourts of the cafés/restaurants listed in shops.json, fountains with moving water, statues/monuments where mapped, flagpoles.
6. The station area and railway: tracks with ballast, sleepers, third rail, buffer stops, the island platform with canopy, benches, signs, ticket machines and validators (keep STATION, TRAINPATH and the hooks the train mission uses), fences along the line, the forecourt. The Alsos park: paths, lawns, benches, playground, lighting.
7. Night: every lamp lights its surroundings convincingly (light pools, glowing lanterns), without real-time lights per lamp.
8. Call placed(kind,x,z,name,src) for every real-world feature you place so tools/verify_placement.js can prove 100% coverage of the map's furniture, crossings, signals, stops and trees. Keep collision (addSeg) correct and never block doors, crossings or the player's spawn.

Budgets: your modules together ≤ ~330k triangles and ≤ ~110 draw calls in a typical street view on medium; use instancing per 192 m cell with distance culling and LOD (see the existing TREECH system), merge static geometry into chunks.

How to verify: for each priority street take street-level shots (both directions, eye height 1.7 m), a low oblique aerial and a top-down, by day and at night, and compare side by side with the reference photos in ref/ and docs/research/img/streets/; fix what differs. Walk test: script the player along Kassaveti, across Platanos square and up to the station for collisions and snagging. Run tools/verify_placement.js when it exists. Zero console errors.
