### What exists now (read it first)
- 10_atlas.js paints 64 tiles of 256² into one 2048² canvas atlas with hand-drawn procedural textures (asphalt, pavers, brick, plaster, facade bays with windows, shop fronts, roof tiles, bark, leaves ...). T maps tile names to indices; RECT[i] is the tile's rectangle; atlasMat() patches MeshLambertMaterial so each vertex carries its tile rect (attribute aRect) and tiles repeat inside their cell (fract + textureGrad), plus a night glow from the tile's alpha (aGlow). Every world module calls GB methods with RECT[T.name] and UVs measured in tile repeats.
- In r147 MeshLambertMaterial is lit per VERTEX. The world looks flat, low-resolution and hand-painted. The owner wants it to look like the real place.
