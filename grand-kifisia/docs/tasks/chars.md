### What to build
1. An asset pipeline in tools/chars/ (scripts, re-runnable) that produces compact, publishable character assets: glTF as .json with embedded base64 buffers, or your own compact .json format; textures as .jpg/.webp/.png files (512² body, 256-512² head is plenty on a phone; hair/eyelash alpha needs an alpha-tested texture). Total download for all people assets: aim for ≤ 14 MB.
   - The owner insists that the ~49 street NPCs "look different from each other". Build a cast of at least 26 visually distinct realistic people (use many different Rocketbox avatars, not recolours of a few; add per-person variation of height/build and, where it looks natural, clothing tint) that fit an affluent Athens suburb on a warm day: casual adults of different ages and builds (both sexes), a couple of business people, a security guard (for the ISA mission), a delivery rider, a chef/waiter for restaurants, a sporty one, 2 children (for the school area), and if it works a dog.
   - Skinning cost matters: facial bones (and for crowd characters, finger bones) can be collapsed into their parents so a crowd character needs ~20-25 bones; the player may keep fingers.
   - Two geometric LODs per avatar (near ≈ 4-8k triangles, far ≈ 1-1.5k) — and replace or keep the old procedural far-LOD as you judge best. On "medium" at most 16 skinned characters are animated at once, about 600k triangles total for the whole frame.
2. A curated animation set (60-90 clips, male and female variants) packed compactly (resample to 20-30 fps, drop facial/nub tracks, quantise; target ≤ 4 MB), covering locomotion with real start/stop/transition clips, several walk styles, idles with fidgets, talking/listening gestures, phone, newspaper, drink, bag, sitting at chair/table, wave, cheer, crouch (sneaking), dancing, taking a photo, pushing a trolley.
   - Rocketbox has no punch, jump, climb-over-fence, basketball shot, knocked-down/lying, getting into a car/driving pose or eating. Author these as hand-keyed clips on the Biped skeleton (key poses, proper easing, anticipation and follow-through, both arms and spine involved) and check them as frame strips. They must not look robotic.
3. An animation system that makes motion look alive:
   - per-character state machine with cross-fades, locomotion blended by speed with phase-synchronised walk/run and playback rate matched to actual ground speed (measure each clip's natural speed from its root motion) so feet do not slide; start, stop and turn transitions; lean into turns and acceleration;
   - additive layers: breathing, head/neck/spine look-at (NPCs glance at the player and look at who they talk to), random fidgets while idle;
   - foot placement on slopes, curbs and steps (Kifisia is hilly: adjust pelvis and feet with two-bone IK, or an honest cheaper approximation);
   - different people move differently: per-character walk style, speed, idle set, posture.
4. Believable street life in 75_npc.js (49+ people, as now): walkers with different styles; people sitting at the REAL cafés/restaurants (use MAP.buildings[].shops with kind café/restaurant/fast_food/bakery and MAP.special door positions; another agent is adding richer POI data later, so keep the seat-finding code in one function) with sit-at-table animations; people on the real benches (MAP.props.bench) reading or on the phone; pairs chatting with talk/listen gestures; people waiting at bus stops (MAP.props.bus), on the station platform and at the Platanos taxi rank; window-shoppers; children near ISA in daytime; the ISA night guard as a Security avatar; a dog walker if the dog works.
