## 1. Kassaveti 19 courtyard — Starbucks + Ralph Lauren + "Πύργος Κασσαβέτη" arcade

Layout (from photos + OSM footprints): a paved courtyard opens off the **north side of Kassaveti** between the Chloe open-air cinema (Kassaveti 17, b-cinema at x 132-161)
and the arcade b423. Stone planters with shrubs at the street edge. Inside, from west to east: **Starbucks pavilion (b426)** — **Ralph Lauren villa (b425)** — **brick arcade (b423)**.
Courtyard floor: grey stone flags / pebble-dash terrazzo panels with a grid of light strips; black cast-iron lantern posts (~3.5 m) with lantern heads; terracotta pots with fan palms.

### 1a. Starbucks — Κασσαβέτη 19 (key `starbucks-kassaveti19`, confidence photo)
- Store #42431-97953 "Kifissia", tel. 210 808 3254, opened 1 Apr 2005; hours Mo–Fr 08:00–23:00, Sa–Su 08:30–23:30 (store locator, Nov 2026). Outdoor seating.
- "Built in a very homey two-storey wood and brick structure ... the former stable of Kasaveti" (Starbucks Everywhere visit #8002, photo 2008) → `img/shops/starbucks_01_courtyard_2008_REFONLY.jpg`.

  "STARBUCKS COFFEE" lettering along the timber fascia under the eave; ground floor = timber French doors between brick/stone piers; a rough stone pier at the corner.
  Square dark-green market umbrellas (#1e6b57; sampled in shade #266c76) with "STARBUCKS COFFEE" valances, dark wooden chairs, brick-red timber planter boxes, a tall tree trunk in the courtyard.

### 1b. Ralph Lauren — Κασσαβέτη 19, villa "Οικία Κότσικα" (OSM way/1076768348, map b425, confidence photo)
- Operator LH Group lists "Ralph Lauren, Kifisia — 19 Kassaveti Str." (franchise page, live Nov 2026). Older press (travelstyle) said the tower-like stone mansion "used to house Teo's hair salon and the Ralph Lauren boutique" — RL is there again now (LH photo with RL awnings).
- Building: protected monument (Wikidata Q122260203 "Kotsikas house", GR-IA13-05-0017). 1910, "an English suburban villa combined with a stone version of the Swiss Cottage style" (archaiologia.gr).
- Photos: `ralphlauren_01_kotsikas_house_day_CCBYSA.jpg` (Commons, C messier, CC BY-SA 4.0 — may ship), `ralphlauren_02_night_lhgroup_REFONLY.jpg`, `ralphlauren_03_night_starbucks_left_travelstyle_REFONLY.jpg`.
- Build spec (front = SSW gable facing Kassaveti, ~17 m back from the kerb):
  - 3 levels: raised ground floor (+1.6 m), first floor, attic in the gable. Eaves ≈ 9.5 m, ridge/finial ≈ 13.5 m. Footprint from OSM (~11 × 17 m).
  - Walls: rough-faced honey/beige limestone ashlar **#c4a68a** (lit side #cbad90, shade #8a7461), rusticated quoins and window surrounds of bigger blocks; white moulded string-course/cornice **#ece8e1** at first-floor level wrapping the bay.
  - Roof: steep (~50°) red-orange clay tiles **#a8553a**; deep white timber bargeboards with carved Swiss-cottage brackets, king-post truss in the apex, turned finial; small pointed dormer on the left slope; lower hipped wing on the left with a white overhanging eave.
  - Front, left half: segment-arched door (~1.4 × 2.8 m, dark grey-blue frame #2f3a40, gold "RALPH LAUREN" on navy in the fanlight) at the top of ~10 white marble steps with curved black wrought-iron rails; curved glass canopy on black scroll brackets.
  - Front, right half: canted 3-sided bay (projects ~1 m) with a large round-arched display window + narrow side lights; on top, a first-floor terrace with a white pierced stone balustrade.
  - First floor: two tall white small-paned casements, each under a navy box awning **#36466a** (the right one lettered "RALPH LAUREN" in gold serif caps). Gable: French window with a white timber balcony on carved brackets.
  - Night: warm up-lights on the stone, interior glow 2700 K.
