## Georeferencing of the aerial
Similarity transform anchored on the OSM fountain node (x −182.07, z −12.73) = aerial pixel (473, 368).
Image "right" = the park's long axis, bearing 15° south of east (matches the OSM outline's long sides); scale 8.1 px/m
(fountain rim 58 px ≈ 7.2 m; road-to-road across the park 680 px ≈ 84 m = OSM width at that section). Park frame: u along the axis
(east +), v across (south +), metres from the fountain. Residual check: the aerial's park edges fall on the OSM outline within ~1.5 m
(preview image `work/lm-park/shots/prev.png`).

## Dimensions
- Outline: 274 × 93 m wedge, 22,855 m² (OSM). Ground: compacted beige earth/gravel (#cdbb9a) everywhere outside the beds.
- Fountain: round basin, outer white rim Ø 7.2 m, kerb 0.45 m high, 8 radial ribs, small central bowl; set in a 4-sector ring of
  hedged quarter-beds (aerial).
- Main walk along the axis through the fountain ~7 m wide; cross walks 3–5 m; walks bounded by clipped box hedges 0.45 m wide,
  0.5–0.6 m high (from shadows in the aerial: ~0.5–0.7 m).
- Beds: 44 traced/inferred beds — 19 lawn (hedged ovals/rectangles), 11 shrub beds (rows of clipped box balls Ø 0.6–1.2 m), 9 tree
  groves (rough planting), 2 palm rows (the 7 + 7 Chamaerops/fan-palm row each side of the west walk), 2 edge strips, 1 rockery.
- Pavilion (south of the fountain): square paved platform ~10.4 × 8.9 m with a round white ribbed dome Ø ~6.2 m (function not verified).
- Small buildings (aerial, function not verified): a single-storey flat-roof service building 6.5 × 5.4 m, a small white two-part booth
  4.2 × 4.9 m NE of the fountain, a single-storey house with red clay tiles 9.9 × 6.4 m near the south edge.
- Trees: 128 — Aleppo pine 30, Melia (bare in the winter aerial) 29, Italian cypress 18, fan palm 14, Atlas cedar 14, plane 9,
  holm oak 8, stone pine 4, pittosporum 1, carob 1 (OSM natural=tree, the registered carob). Crown diameters measured on the aerial;
  species identified from crown shape/colour/winter leaf state in the aerial — this is an estimate, not a survey.
- Benches: 28 (wood slats on dark cast-iron ends, 1.8 m), along the main walks facing the walk; 8 lamps (dark green post, opal globes,
  the same type as the Kefalari grove photo r04 — inferred, not visible in the aerial).
- Water tank (r07): riveted green iron cylinder Ø ~4.6 m × ~3.6 m, on 8 cast-iron columns ~3 m high, inscription band
  "ΚΗΦΙΣΙΑ ΑΛΣΟΣ" with Σ.Α. monograms around the bottom ring, ladder/pipe and valve wheel on the side; position near the NE tip
  estimated (photo geotag + PLACES.md).
- ALSOS CAFE BAR (OSM amenity=cafe): look inferred (single-storey pavilion with terrace of bistro tables) — no photo.
