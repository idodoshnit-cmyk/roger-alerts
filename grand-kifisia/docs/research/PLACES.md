## Πλατεία Πλατάνου (Platanos Square)  (`platanos_square`, x=-66, z=5, lat 38.072655, lon 23.811748)

Kifisia's central square on the east side of Kifisias Avenue at the bottom of Kassaveti, named after its old plane tree. The 'square' is a planted, elongated traffic island (OSM landuse=grass, 57 x 14 m, long axis NW-SE) with the plane tree near its north end, a decorative municipal fountain at its south end, a row of trees along its east edge, and the one-way slip road 'Plateia Platanou' running round it. Bus stops PLATEIA PLATANOU (with shelter and bench, line 71 etc.) and PLATEIA KIFISIAS are at its edges; the Alsos (grove) begins across the slip road to the west and the Platanos underground car park (621 spaces, Adrianou 6) lies to the south-west. Signalised zebra crossings link it to the bottom of Kassaveti. The north side of Kassaveti's first block is the long neoclassical building Kassaveti 1 (HSBC on the ground floor) in front of which the yellow taxis queue along the kerb (photo r11).

## Σταθμός Κηφισιάς (Kifisia station, Line 1 terminus)  (`station`, x=-375, z=-100.8, lat 38.073608, lon 23.808225)

In front: a forecourt with taxi rank, bus terminus (AFETIRIA ST. KIFISIAS), bicycle stands and two lit decorative fountains, edged by dark-green steel railings with diagonal-cross panels.

Venue of the annual Flower Show (Anthokomiki Ekthesi, ~20 stremmata, late April to May) with rows of growers' stalls.
