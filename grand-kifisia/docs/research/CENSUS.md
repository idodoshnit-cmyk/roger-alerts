## What implementers need to know (read this first)

- **Build tiers A and B** (`tier` field). That is the "build set": every entry there gets a shopfront / door plate.
  Tier C = an old or weak listing whose existence today is unknown (use at most for a vacant "ΕΝΟΙΚΙΑΖΕΤΑΙ" front, or ignore).
  Tier X = reported closed by its source (do not build as an open shop).
- `streetLevel:false` means an upper-floor business (doctor, lawyer, accountant, architect, office): a brass door plate at the
  building entrance, not a shop window. `streetLevel:true` = a shopfront at pavement level is likely. `null` = unknown, treat as shopfront.
- Position: `x,z` are game metres (`x = (lon − 23.8125) × 87716`, `z = −(lat − 38.0727) × 110998`), the same projection as `kifisia_map.json`.
  `posErrM` is an honest estimate of the position error. OSM survey points are good to a few metres; everything else is
  typically 10–30 m off along the street (see "Position quality"). So: put the shop on the nearest building facade that faces
  `street`, in the house-number order given in the tables below, rather than at the exact point.
- `osm` is set when the business is the same as an OpenStreetMap object (node/way id) — those are the places the map already knows.
- `name` is the name as the source spells it (Greek where the sign is Greek); `nameLatin` is a Latin form for the sign/HUD
  (the brand's own spelling for chains, else a transliteration).
- Extra fields when known: `phone`, `website`, `brand`, `lastSeen` (year of the newest evidence), `inPlayable`, `inFocus`
  (inside the dense centre: Kassaveti/Levidou/Kolokotroni/Kyriazi/Kifisias/Platanos/station), `spread` (position spread across sources was large).

## Method

1. **OpenStreetMap** (`kif.osm`, bbox 23.804,38.0675 → 23.8265,38.0795): every node/way with shop, amenity (commercial values only:
   café, restaurant, fast_food, bar, pharmacy, bank, …), office, craft, tourism, leisure (commercial), healthcare plus a name or brand.
   OSM objects are tier A (a mapper saw them). Also extracted: the `addr:housenumber` points and the street centre lines, used by the geocoder.
2. **Overture Maps places** (fetched 2026-11-18 by the data agent; CDLA-Permissive-2.0). Overture's own `confidence`
   and its source list (Meta/Microsoft) are kept; Overture confidence ≥ ~0.45 counts as a fresh listing.
3. **Foursquare OS Places** (release 2025-02-06, Apache-2.0, read from the public mirror and filtered to the bbox).
   `date_closed` → tier X; `date_refreshed` older than ~2021 → tier C unless confirmed by another source.
4. **Web**: chain store locators and directories for named brands (`tools/census/web_entries.json`, each with its URL).
   This layer is the one still growing. Hand-checked corrections (aliases such as ΕΤΕ = Εθνική Τράπεζα, rebrands such as
   HSBC → CrediaBank at Kassaveti 1) live in `tools/census/overrides.json`; a rebranded entry carries `rebrandedFrom` + `rebrandNote`.
4b. **vrisko.gr** (the Greek yellow pages; `tools/census/08_vrisko.py`): the Kifisia listings of the shopfront categories (slugs tried and result counts: `work/research-census/raw/vrisko/probe.txt`)
   (clothes, shoes, jewellery, cosmetics, opticians, pharmacies, banks, kiosks, cafés, bars, restaurants, hairdressers, beauty, florists,
   supermarkets, butchers, bookshops, toys, hotels, travel agents …), read from the schema.org data in the public category pages
   (robots.txt allows `/dir/`; one request every 3 s). Paid adverts from other suburbs are ignored. Names are registered company names,
   so the sign name is taken from them ("STARBUCKS COFFEE - ALSHAYA HELLAS ΑΕ" → "STARBUCKS COFFEE"); a bare owner's name becomes
   category + surname ("Φαρμακείο Μπίσιας", "Περίπτερο Λώλης"), which is how such shops are signed. A vrisko listing is a current
   registration: alone it makes tier B, together with another source it raises confidence (+0.2). Its coordinates are rough
   (median 27 m from the OSM point of the same business), so its address is what places it. Entries "inside" an arcade/mall/hotel get
   `inside:true, streetLevel:false`; counters inside a department store (e.g. Estée Lauder inside Hondos Center) are dropped.
5. **Merge** (`tools/census/05_merge.py`): names normalised (Greek accents, Greek↔Latin transliteration, chain aliases);
   two entries are the same business when the normalised names match within 40 m, or when they share street + number and
   have similar names. Position preference: OSM → address geocoded on OSM house numbers → Foursquare → Overture → vrisko.
6. **Geocoder** (`tools/census/addr.py`): for each street, the OSM house-number points are projected onto the street axis;
   odd and even numbers are separate sides; a number is placed by linear interpolation between the nearest known
   numbers on the same side, then pushed to the facade line on that side. Leave-one-out tests on the OSM numbers give the error
   estimates written to `posErrM`.
7. **Categories** (`tools/census/cats.py`): every source category is mapped to one normalised list (clothes, shoes, jewellery,
   cosmetics, optician, pharmacy, bank, cafe, restaurant, fast_food, bakery, supermarket, kiosk, hairdresser, beauty, bookshop,
   electronics, telecom, hotel, clinic, doctor, dentist, real_estate, gallery, office, …).

Before this (versions up to 202611210845) the even numbers 244–256 of Kifisias Avenue were placed up to 380 m too far north.

Reading: a published Foursquare/Overture coordinate is typically 12–15 m from the real door, one in ten more than 30 m.
After geocoding / facade snapping the census median is about 11 m. This is good enough to put a shop on the right block and
the right side of the street; it is not good enough to choose between two neighbouring doors — use the house-number order.

## What "complete" means here

This file contains every business that at least one open source places inside the play area, de-duplicated, with an honest
statement of how sure we are that it exists today (`tier`, `confidence`, `open`). It is **not** a ground survey: a shop that
opened in 2026 and is in no open dataset is missing, and some tier B entries will have closed. On the main shopping streets
the open sources together are dense, so building tiers A + B there gives streets that match reality far better than OSM alone.

constant is `87745.7` (at lat 38.0727); changing it would move the east edge of the map by about 0.4 m — not worth a rebuild.
